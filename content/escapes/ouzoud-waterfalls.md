# Ouzoud Waterfalls Experience 💦🇲🇦

Escape Marrakech for a spectacular day in the Moroccan countryside and discover the famous Ouzoud Waterfalls, surrounded by olive groves, red cliffs and cascading waters.

---

## Experience Overview
- **Duration:** Full day — approximately 10–11 hours
- **Departure:** Marrakech (Private hotel/riad pickup & return)
- **Type:** 100% Private experience for your group only
- **Pricing:** Tailored quote via WhatsApp (depends on season and guest count)

## What's Included
- 🚐 Private air-conditioned hotel/riad pickup & return transfer
- 💦 Visit the spectacular 110-meter Ouzoud Waterfalls
- 🥾 Guided walk through olive groves and canyon paths around the falls
- 🐒 Opportunity to meet the wild native Barbary macaque monkeys
- 🛶 Traditional wooden boat crossing at the foot of the waterfalls
- 🌿 Scenic Moroccan countryside drive & Atlas foothills viewpoints
- 🍽️ Traditional Moroccan lunch overlooking the cascading falls
- 🫖 Authentic Moroccan mint tea and local cultural experience
- 📸 Free time to explore, swim (seasonal), and take photos
